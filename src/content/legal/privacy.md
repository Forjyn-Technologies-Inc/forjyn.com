Forjyn Technologies Inc. (“Forjyn”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates a mobile application and website (the “Service”) designed to connect investors and startups and facilitate investment discovery, communication, and due diligence activities.

Forjyn is incorporated as a **for-profit corporation** in Ontario, Canada.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard personal information in accordance with Canadian privacy laws, including the **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)**, applicable provincial requirements, and mobile platform policies.

Where required by applicable law, we obtain consent for the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information. By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope of the Service

The Service allows users to:

- Discover investment opportunities
- Share startup information with investors
- Communicate through in-app messaging
- Upload and review due diligence materials
- Facilitate connections between investors and startups

Forjyn Technologies Inc. operates solely as a **technology platform**.

Forjyn:

- does not invest capital,
- does not issue securities,
- does not provide investment advice,
- does not perform investment suitability assessments, and
- does not conduct or certify due diligence.

Forjyn does not participate in negotiation, recommendation, solicitation, or execution of securities transactions between users.

The platform may promote investment opportunities, including venture capital funds that are separate legal entities from Forjyn.

Forjyn is not registered with the Ontario Securities Commission and does not act as a dealer, advisor, investment fund manager, or underwriter.

Forjyn performs identity verification services requested by participating investment organizations and AML support functions solely for platform administration purposes. Such information may also be accessed by authorized users of the platform, including general partner entities responsible for their own regulatory compliance obligations. All securities compliance responsibilities remain with the applicable issuer or fund entity.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials.

## 8A. Data Breach Notification

While we implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.


If we become aware of a security incident involving personal information, we will investigate the incident promptly and take reasonable steps to contain, assess, and remediate the issue.


Where required by applicable law, we will notify affected individuals and applicable regulatory authorities of a breach involving personal information where it is determined that the breach creates a real risk of significant harm or where notification is otherwise required by law.


Where appropriate, notifications may include:


- a description of the nature of the incident;
- the categories of personal information involved;
- the steps we have taken or intend to take to mitigate the incident;
- recommendations regarding actions affected individuals may take to reduce potential harm; and
- contact information for obtaining additional information.

We maintain records of security incidents in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

## 13. Automated Processing and Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence ("AI") and other automated technologies are integrated throughout the Service to assist users in evaluating opportunities, organizing information, reviewing documents, and improving overall platform functionality.

Depending on the features used, AI tools may process information voluntarily submitted to the Service, including but not limited to:

- user profile information;
- startup profiles and business information;
- investor profile information;
- documents uploaded to due diligence rooms;
- pitch decks, financial information, cap tables, and business materials;
- messages exchanged through the platform;
- notes or written responses submitted by users; and
- where enabled by the user, call recordings, transcripts, or meeting notes uploaded for analysis.

AI functionality may be used to generate summaries, answer questions about uploaded due diligence materials using information contained within those materials, identify relevant information across documents, organize content, provide contextual insights, assist with due diligence reviews, suggest potential matches between users, and support other analytical features made available through the Service. 

To provide these features, submitted information may be securely processed by trusted third-party AI service providers acting on our behalf. These providers process information only for the purpose of delivering the requested AI functionality and in accordance with applicable contractual, privacy, and security obligations.

Where users upload recordings, transcripts, or other materials containing information about third parties, the uploading user is responsible for ensuring that all necessary permissions and legally required consents have been obtained before submitting such information to the Service.

AI-generated outputs are intended solely as informational and decision-support tools. They may contain inaccuracies, omissions, or outdated information and should not be relied upon as legal, financial, investment, tax, accounting, or other professional advice. Users remain solely responsible for reviewing all AI-generated content and making their own independent decisions.

AI-generated outputs are not used to make fully automated decisions regarding investment eligibility, regulatory compliance, platform access, account status, or other decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects without meaningful human involvement.

We may use aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized information to improve and evaluate our AI systems and platform functionality. Forjyn does not use Customer Content to train publicly available artificial intelligence models.

By using AI-enabled features within the Service, you acknowledge that information you voluntarily submit may be processed using artificial intelligence technologies for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.
