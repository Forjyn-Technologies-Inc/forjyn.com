## Our Commitment

Forjyn Technologies Inc. ("Forjyn," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to providing a website and technology platform that are accessible and inclusive for all users, including individuals with disabilities.

We recognize that accessibility is an ongoing process and are committed to continually improving the accessibility, usability, and overall user experience of our website and Platform.

## Accessibility Standards

Our goal is to design, develop, and maintain our website and Platform in a manner that is consistent with recognized accessibility best practices, including the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA**, where reasonably practicable.

Where applicable, we also seek to support the accessibility objectives of the **Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 (AODA)** and other applicable accessibility legislation.

## Accessibility Features

When developing and maintaining the Platform, we strive to incorporate accessibility features such as:

- Clear and consistent navigation.
- Keyboard-accessible functionality where reasonably practicable.
- Appropriate heading structures and semantic HTML.
- Alternative text for meaningful images.
- Sufficient colour contrast to improve readability.
- Responsive layouts that function across a variety of devices and screen sizes.
- Compatibility with commonly used assistive technologies where reasonably practicable.
- Readable typography and scalable text.
- Clearly labelled forms and interactive elements.

As our Platform evolves, accessibility improvements may be implemented on an ongoing basis.

## Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort rather than a one-time initiative.

We regularly evaluate opportunities to improve accessibility as we introduce new features, update existing functionality, and respond to user feedback.

Because our Platform includes integrations with third-party services and technologies, certain accessibility features may be affected by systems that are not owned or controlled by Forjyn. While we encourage our service providers to support accessible experiences, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party content or services.

## Feedback and Assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing any portion of our website or Platform, encounter an accessibility barrier, or have suggestions for improving accessibility, we encourage you to contact us.

When possible, please include:

- the webpage or feature you were attempting to access;
- a description of the issue you experienced;
- the device, browser, or assistive technology you were using; and
- your preferred method of contact.

We will make reasonable efforts to investigate reported accessibility issues and provide an appropriate response.
